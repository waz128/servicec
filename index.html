Services rarely fail because of technology alone.

They fail because ownership becomes unclear, knowledge leaves with people, suppliers operate independently and operational complexity grows faster than an organisation's ability to manage it.

Monitoring often shows healthy infrastructure while customers remain unable to consume the services they depend on.

I help organisations restore clarity, stability and confidence in the services that keep their business running.

My work spans service recovery, operational maturity, governance, process design, service observability and operating model development across enterprise and government environments.

  • Improve service and operational maturity.

  • Improve service observability and business outcome visibility.

Typical Situations

  • Critical operational knowledge has left the organisation.
  • Documentation no longer reflects reality.
  • Service ownership is unclear.
  • Multiple suppliers operate independently.

  • Major incidents are becoming normal business operations.
  • Service performance has deteriorated and customer confidence is declining.

  • Services need to transition successfully into operations.
